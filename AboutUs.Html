About Ann's Bakery

Our Story

Ann's Bakery began as a dream in a small home kitchen. Ann always had a gift for baking - her scones were legendary among family and friends, and her birthday cakes became the highlight of every celebration.

What started as baking for loved ones quickly grew into a word-of-mouth business. Today, Ann's Bakery serves the local community with the same love and care that started it all.

Our Mission

"To bring the joy of freshly baked, unforgettable goods that are worth the wait - where quality, passion, and locally sourced ingredients come together in every bite."

Ann's Baking Journey

  • Formal Training: Ann studied baking at a recognised culinary school, where she honed her skills and developed her signature techniques.
  • Passion-Driven: Baking is not just a job for Ann - it is her gift and her joy. Every batch is made with care and attention.
  • Local Ingredients: Ann sources her ingredients locally, supporting other small businesses and ensuring the freshest flavours.
  • Quality Over Speed: Ann believes that good things take time. She takes a little longer than a typical bakery, but her customers agree - it's worth the wait.

What Makes Ann's Bakery Different

Worth the Wait

In a world of fast food and quick fixes, Ann takes her time to get things right. Her scones are light and buttery, her cakes are beautifully designed, and every order receives her full attention.

Locally Sourced, Special Ingredients

Ann uses special, locally sourced ingredients that make her baked goods stand out. She believes in supporting local farmers and businesses while delivering the best flavour to her customers.

Personal Touch

Because Ann works alone, she knows many of her customers personally. This allows her to understand their preferences, remember their favourite orders, and ensure every customer feels valued.

Our Values

  • Quality First - We never compromise on quality. Every item is baked fresh and with care.
  • Local Ingredients - We support local suppliers and use the freshest ingredients available.
  • Worth the Wait - Good things take time, and our customers agree it's always worth it.
  • Passion in Every Bite - Baking is our passion, and you can taste it in every product.
